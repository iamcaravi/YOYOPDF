Frequently asked questions

What does color inversion do exactly?

It flips every pixel's color to its negative — light areas become dark and colors shift to their opposites.

What is this actually useful for?

Common uses include creating a quick negative-style effect, checking a light-on-dark version of a graphic for a dark-mode UI, or reversing a scanned negative image back toward its original colors.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No, the pixel inversion happens locally on your device.
